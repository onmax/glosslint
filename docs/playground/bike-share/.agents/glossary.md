# Glossary

The words the bike-share app, its translations and its help pages use.

## Dock
What: A powered stand where one bike is locked.
Avoid:
- rack: Racks are unpowered.

## eBike
What: A bike with a motor that helps you pedal.

## Day Pass (DP)
What: Unlimited 30-minute rides until midnight.
Case: context

## Rider
What: A person with an account who can unlock bikes.
Notes: Staff who move bikes are crew, not riders.
Avoid: customer
