# Glossary

<!-- Format and checks: https://glosslint.onmax.me/guide/glossary -->

The words the rider app and the help center use.

Presets: tech, inclusive
Allow: check out our, roof rack

## Station
What: A group of docks on one street corner.
Avoid:
- hub: Riders confuse it with the depot.
Links:
- [Stations](https://example.com/docs/stations)

## Rider
What: A person with an account who can unlock bikes.
How: Staff who move bikes are crew, not riders.
Avoid: customer

## Ride
What: The time from unlocking a bike to docking it.
Avoid: trip

## Week Pass (WP)
What: Unlimited 30-minute rides for seven days.
Avoid: weekly ticket
Links:
- [Passes](https://example.com/docs/passes)
Translations:
- fr: keep (abonnement hebdomadaire)

## kiosk
What: The screen at a station where you buy passes.
How: Always lowercase.
Avoid: terminal
Case: exact

## Dock
What: A powered stand where one bike is locked.
How: A station is a group of docks.
Avoid:
- rack: Racks are unpowered.
- bay
Good:
- Return the bike to any dock
Bad:
- Return the bike to any rack
Links:
- [Docks](https://example.com/docs/docks)
Translations:
- fr: borne
Forms:
- fr: bornes
Hints:
- fr: Feminine: la borne.
